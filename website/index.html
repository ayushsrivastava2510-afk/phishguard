What types of threats can PhishGuard intercept and analyze? ▼
PhishGuard protects across dual vectors: Enterprise Email (zero-day spear-phishing, Business Email Compromise, lookalike domains, and malicious attachments) and Mobile SMS / Smishing (banking KYC scams, electricity cut-off threats, and commercial messages dispatched from unauthorized SIMs in violation of TRAI DLT regulations).
How does PhishGuard stop attackers from sending more phishing attacks? ▼
PhishGuard transitions from passive detection to active prevention: it generates perimeter blocklist scripts for Microsoft 365 Exchange Online and Linux Postfix to reject attacks at the edge, drafts formal ICANN RFC 2142 domain revocation notices to take down attacker sites, hardens DNS with strict DMARC p=reject enforcement, and compiles DoT Sanchar Saathi dossiers to deactivate scammer SIM cards.
Why does the DistilBERT Transformer outperform traditional ML algorithms? ▼
Traditional algorithms (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression) evaluate keywords in isolation and fail on ~46% of sophisticated Business Email Compromise (BEC) attacks. PhishGuard's 66M-parameter DistilBERT transformer evaluates deep bidirectional linguistic syntax and conversational coercion, achieving ~98.6% accuracy in under 100ms on standard CPU hardware.
How does the Smishing Sentinel avoid flagging personal messages from friends? ▼
PhishGuard deploys a Person-to-Person (P2P) Exemption Engine. When an SMS arrives from a 10-digit mobile SIM, PhishGuard checks for coercive banking panic cues, suspicious payment URLs, or APK download links. Casual messages between friends without financial coercion are safely verified at 5/100 (Safe).
Is sensitive enterprise email or SMS data transmitted to any third-party cloud API? ▼
No. PhishGuard runs 100% offline-first. All parsing, hashing, transformer inference, and evidence generation occur locally inside your security perimeter or SOC server. No data ever leaves your device.
